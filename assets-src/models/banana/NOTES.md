# The banana

Why the shape is what it is; the numbers are in the file and the rules are in
the tests.

- **A clump, not a stalk.** Three pseudostems of stepped ages, broader than it
  is tall — the opposite proportion to the palm, which is what tells the two
  apart when both are a few pixels.
- **Leaves too big to be believed, and bigger still than that.** A banana at
  this distance is *only* its leaves; a first pass at half the width read as a
  spray of grass.
- **Young leaves stand, old ones have flopped.** Held at one angle it reads as
  a shuttlecock; the spread of rises gives it a top and a skirt.
- **A raised midrib with the edges falling away** — the opposite fold to the
  palm's keeled frond, and what the eye knows a banana leaf by.
- **The bunch is a pixel, and it stays.** Nothing else in the world is that
  red-purple, so at any zoom showing it at all the clump is unmistakable.
- **Leaf green lighter and yellower than the ground it stands on**, which runs
  from forest to grass — a green that joined in with the grass left a clump
  reading as a bush-shaped patch of it. The stems are paler and greyer than
  their own leaves, so the clump has a core to read against dark ground.
- **Triangles, not quads.** A swept ring twists, and a non-planar quad gets one
  normal in Blender and two differently-lit triangles at export.
- **The tallest stem's foot is at the origin.**

Where bananas *grow* is `crates/world/src/bananas.rs`.
