# The scalesia

Why the shape is what it is; the numbers are in the file and the rules are in
the tests.

- **A dome on a stick, and that is the whole silhouette.** A slender trunk
  most of the tree's height, then one rounded crown. Nothing else here has
  that: a palm is a stick with fronds radiating off the top, a mangrove is a
  dome too but low and on legs, and the banana and the cactus have no trunk
  to speak of. At a distance where all five are proportions, this is the tall
  one with a round head.
- **Taller than the palm, and the trunk is most of it.** The real tree is a
  daisy that grew to fifteen metres in a hurry, with the leaves all at the
  top, and the proportion is what says so. A shorter trunk under the same
  crown read as a bush.
- **The trunk leans, kinks, and forks into three limbs no two alike**, for
  the reason every trunk here leans: the server only turns a plant about the
  vertical, and a stand of straight ones is a row of posts.
- **One crown of many small lumps on the golden angle**, the mangrove's
  construction — see its NOTES — because it is the one that never comes back
  round on itself. Three of the lumps sit on the limb ends so the limbs hold
  something up; the rest fill one dome over them.
- **The lowest lumps are a shade darker**, which gives the crown a lit top
  and a shaded underside without any facet carrying a gradient.
- **Leaf grey-green, and paler than any ground in its country.** The humid
  shoulders are painted in jungle, canopy and forest — the darkest greens in
  the world — so where the mangrove is the dark thing on mid-toned marsh, this
  is the pale thing on dark ground. It is also what the real leaves are:
  grey-green and felted, not glossy.
- **The bark is pale**, lichened grey-brown rather than the palm's timber, so
  the trunk reads against the jungle floor it stands on.
- **Five white pips on top.** *Scalesia* flowers are white daisy heads, and
  at this distance a flowering crown is a few pale pixels on a green dome.
  Kept for the same reason the banana keeps its bunch: nothing else in this
  world is that colour in that place.
- **Five-sided trunk, four-sided limbs, eight-triangle lumps.** A facet has to
  be big enough to read as a facet.
- **The foot ring lies flat** whatever the trunk's lean, so the foot is at the
  origin, which is where the client puts it on the height field.

Where scalesia *stand* is `crates/world/src/scalesia.rs`.
