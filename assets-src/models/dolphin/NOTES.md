# The dolphin

Why the shape is what it is; the numbers are in the file and the rules are in
the tests.

- **Seen for a second at a time**: an arc through the surface, most of the body
  under water the rest of the while. Nothing here is for a stopped, close-up
  dolphin.
- **Life size, not scaled up.** A pod leaps close enough to the boat that an
  outsized dolphin would read as a whale, which is a different promise.
- **Horizontal flukes and a raked dorsal, which is the whole species.** A
  vertical tail or an upright fin reads as a shark from every angle that
  matters, and surface-piercing angles are all this model has.
- **Girth forward of the middle, spine rising aft.** Six-corner rings are the
  fewest that read as round rather than boxy when the back breaks the surface,
  and the lifting centre-line gives the leap its curve though the mesh is
  straight.
- **A pale belly.** A pod leaves the water on its side as often as level, and
  one colour all over lost the whole shape of the leap.
- **Origin amidships**, where a real dolphin bends: the game pitches the model
  about it to follow the arc, so anywhere else and the nose sweeps.

Where a pod *swims* is the server's word; what it looks like doing so is
`crates/game/src/beasts.rs`.
