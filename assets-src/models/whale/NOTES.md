# The whale

Why the shape is what it is; the numbers are in the file and the rules are in
the tests.

- **Seen as a back**: a long dark mass shouldering through the surface for a
  few seconds at a time. Everything is in the top line of the silhouette.
- **Life size, and the scale is the animal.** It reads as a whale because it is
  so much longer than the dolphins and the boat the player has been taught to
  expect. Bigger would be a sea monster, which is a different promise.
- **Girth peaks well forward and the spine rises aft** — a rorqual's lines, so
  what breaks the surface is an arc visibly going somewhere rather than a log.
- **The dorsal fin is small and far aft.** Tall and amidships reads as an orca,
  and an orca circling a sailing boat is a mood this world is not selling.
- **Horizontal flukes**: vertical means shark.
- **A pale belly**, because a whale that was one colour was a slab from every
  angle, and the flukes coming up are the moment the shape wants reading.
- **Origin amidships**, where the surfacing arc pivots.

Where a whale *swims* is the server's word; what it looks like doing so is
`crates/game/src/beasts.rs`.
