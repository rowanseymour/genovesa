# The eagle

Why the shape is what it is; the numbers are in the file and the rules are in
the tests.

- **Only ever seen circling a summit**, against sky or against rock, never
  landing and never approached. It is a soaring silhouette and nothing else.
- **Twice life size.** At the distance a summit is watched from, an honest
  golden eagle is a flyspeck — and twice is still small enough that nobody
  sails under one and laughs.
- **The wings kink at the wrist and rise past it.** That dihedral is most of
  what the silhouette *is* from below or edge-on; flat boards read as a glider.
  Two panels a side is the fewest that can hold the kink.
- **The tail is a fan and the head barely exists**, so the shape has a front
  and no more.
- **Dark above, pale below, white head and tail.** The white is the concession
  to being four pixels across: seen from above, an underside never shows, so
  countershading alone would leave a dark blob. White at both ends of a dark
  bird is the one pattern that survives being that small, and it is what a fish
  eagle looks like. Blocks, never feathers — real plumage colour at this size
  only muddies the shape.

Where an eagle *flies* is `crates/game/src/wildlife.rs`; nothing about it
crosses the wire.
